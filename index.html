About Nikal Technologies

A regional platform for projects that last

Headquartered in Nairobi with branches in Kampala, Dar es Salaam and Garowe, Nikal Technologies Limited develops, finances and delivers projects across East Africa, from utility-scale renewable energy and heavy industry to urban development and government technology.

Our mission is to transform industries through innovative project development, strategic financial solutions and advanced technology expertise, bridging the gap between vision and reality and delivering value that lasts for generations.

Finance

Project funding, structuring and Sharia-compliant instruments.

Heavy Industries

Infrastructure, markets, mining and civil works at regional scale.

Renewable Energy

Solar, wind, hydro, BESS and cogeneration from concept to EPC.

Technology

AI, cybersecurity, data centers, edtech and agritech platforms.
